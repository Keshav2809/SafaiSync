♻️ The 3 Rs

1. Reduce
Carry a cloth bag and bottle, refuse single-use plastic, buy only what you need.
2. Reuse
Reuse jars, bottles and boxes, repair before replacing, donate old clothes and books.
3. Recycle
Give clean dry waste to authorised collectors and drop e-waste at designated centres.

✅ Do

  • Keep wet and dry waste in separate bins
  • Rinse containers before putting them in the dry bin
  • Wrap broken glass and sharp items in paper
  • Hand over waste to the collection vehicle on time
  • Report overflowing bins using SafaiSync

🚫 Don't

  • Don't burn garbage, it releases toxic smoke
  • Don't throw waste in drains, rivers or open plots
  • Don't mix medicines, batteries or tubelights with household waste
  • Don't put food waste in plastic bags
  • Don't litter from vehicles or while walking
TRY AT HOME

🌱 Make compost in 4 steps

1. Pick a pot
Use a bucket or pot with small holes for air and drainage.
2. Layer it
Alternate kitchen scraps (green) with dry leaves or paper (brown).
3. Stir weekly
Mix to add air and keep it slightly moist, not soggy.
4. Use it
In 6 to 8 weeks you get dark, earthy compost for plants.

💡 Myth vs Fact

MYTH Wet and dry waste can go in one bin, the vehicle mixes it anyway.
FACT Segregation at source makes recycling and composting possible.
MYTH Burning waste is the quickest way to get rid of it.
FACT It pollutes air and harms lungs, especially for children and elders.
MYTH My small bit of litter doesn't matter.
FACT Millions of small actions add up, and so do millions of small habits.
